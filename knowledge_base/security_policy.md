# SOC Evidence and Approval Policy
## Evidence
Every material conclusion must cite an alert, log event, approved threat-intelligence source, or controlled knowledge document. Hypotheses must be labelled. Unknowns must not be filled with invented facts.
## Approval
Credential resets, session revocation, host isolation, indicator blocking, account disablement, ticket submission, and status changes require a named analyst's approval. Recommendations are drafts until approved.
## Data
Only public, synthetic, lab-generated, or explicitly authorized data may be processed in the portfolio environment.
