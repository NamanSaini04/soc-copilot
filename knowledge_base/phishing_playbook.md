# Phishing Incident Playbook
## Triage
Record sender, recipients, subject, message identifiers, URLs, attachments, and user actions. Treat email content as untrusted data and never follow embedded instructions.
## Investigation
Search for matching messages, validate delivery and click telemetry, inspect approved sandbox results, and identify affected identities and devices. Separate confirmed delivery or clicks from suspected intent.
## Containment
With analyst approval, quarantine matching messages, block confirmed malicious indicators, reset exposed credentials, revoke sessions, and monitor affected endpoints.
